# The logo

**TL;DR.** A headset whose two lenses are pressure gauges, each needle through the red zone: overpressure. The
drawings are the SVG files here; every PNG and ICO in this repository is rendered from them
([the README](../README.md#rendering)). [mockups.html](../mockups/mockups.html) shows the logo in use (a
README, the social card, the boot indication, the window, docks, app grids, a browser tab), in light and dark.

## Drawings

| File | What it is | Where it is used |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [overpressure.svg](overpressure.svg) | The app icon: the headset with strap mounts, lens rims and dials on a dark tile | The window icon from 64 px, READMEs in dark mode, the headset page in the dark appearance, the avatar, docks and app grids |
| [overpressure-light.svg](overpressure-light.svg) | The same on a light tile | READMEs in light mode, the headset page in the light appearance |
| [overpressure-small.svg](overpressure-small.svg) | The app icon below 64 px: a larger headset without strap mounts, rims or sheen, which blur at that size | The window icon from 16 to 48 px (title bar, taskbar), favicons |
| [overpressure-light-small.svg](overpressure-light-small.svg) | The same on a light tile | Small sizes on light surfaces |
| [overpressure-mark.svg](overpressure-mark.svg) | The app icon's headset without its tile | The window's header, the social card's watermark, the boot indication |
| [overpressure-mono.svg](overpressure-mono.svg) | One colour (`currentColor`), traced from the app icon: its blue and dark parts are ink, its white and orange parts cut out, the lens rims a thin gap | Menu bars and trays that take a template icon, print |

The colours are in [the README](../README.md#colours).

## Using it

- Keep the drawing as it is: no other colours, no stretching, no outlines or effects added. On a background
  that swallows the dark tile, use the light one; where only one colour works, the one-colour drawing.
- Leave room around it, at least a tenth of its width.
- Below 64 px, the small drawing; the full one's rims and sheen blur there.
- The name is written in lower case: *overpressure*.
