# The name and the logo

**TL;DR.** The files here are under [CC BY-SA 4.0](LICENSE): you may share and adapt them, with credit and under
the same license. The name *overpressure* and its logo also stand for the project, so use them to talk about
overpressure, not to look like it. This page describes how the project asks them to be used. It isn't legal
advice.

## Welcome

- Writing, talking or making videos about overpressure, with its name and logo.
- Linking to the project, its releases or its chat with the logo as the link's picture.
- A community, a group or a talk about overpressure that says it isn't run by the project unless it is.
- Stickers, wallpapers and other things for your own use or to give away.

## Please don't

- Use the name or the logo for something the project doesn't make, so that it looks like overpressure's: a fork,
  an installer, an image, an app, a store page, a support service or an account.
- Use them for anything sold, unless the project agreed in an issue first.
- Change the logo and present it as the project's.

A fork is welcome under its own name and its own logo, saying that it is based on overpressure.

## Questions

Ask in an issue in [overpressure/brand](https://github.com/overpressure/brand/issues), or in the chat
([#overpressure:matrix.org](https://matrix.to/#/#overpressure:matrix.org)).
